# Website - Dr. Romain di Stasi

This repository contains the source code for my academic website.

The website provides an overview of my academic profile, including:

- Research interests and academic background  
- Ongoing research projects, with links to their respective websites (e.g., CUBE-SD, EmoLearn, EvoPlayBox)  
- Publications (peer-reviewed articles, preprints)  
- Talks, seminars, and conference presentations  
- Grants and research funding  
- Academic service and other professional activities  

On this website, I regularly upload the PDFs and R scripts for my courses. These materials are available in the “Training” or “Teaching” sections, depending on the target audience. In addition, poster presentations from interns or Master’s students may be made available, provided that I have obtained their agreement.
